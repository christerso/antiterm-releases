# AntiTerm 1.1.0

- Pictures open in a split: `open photo.jpg` shows a PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or BMP where a film would play. Zoom and move it smoothly, adjust it, crop and scale it, undo, save a copy, and copy it with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac).
- AntiMail's approvals show as a card in the split where Claude runs, answered only by your own click or keys.
- Every answer AntiTerm types for you is marked on its line and listed in the Answers log.
- Notes from AntiMail reach the notepad as soon as they are posted.
- A real sun with a soft flare in the planetarium, a calmer deep sea, and a black hole that comes nearer with each new mail.
- Pictures behind the splits without bands of colour, and a close button in Preferences.
- antish: the ghosted suggestion shows at once, and declarations take NAME=value whole.
- The prompt reads a repository's state from its files, with no git or jj started.
- Fixed: dragging to select text shows the selection again; no console windows on Windows; on a Mac, no program started every second and a half to find the agents.
